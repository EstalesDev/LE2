About Us

The University of Saint La Salle is a Catholic educational institution committed to providing quality education rooted in Lasallian values of faith, service, and communion in mission. We strive to form individuals who are not only academically excellent but also morally responsible and socially aware.Founded with the vision of holistic education, the university continues to uphold its tradition of academic excellence through innovative teaching, research, and community engagement. We offer a wide range of academic programs that prepare students for meaningful careers and lifelong learning.

Our community is dedicated to nurturing students in a safe and supportive environment where they are encouraged to grow in knowledge, character, and leadership. We believe that education is a powerful tool for transformation and service to others.
