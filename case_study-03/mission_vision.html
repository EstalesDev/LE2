Mission & Vision

Our Mission

The University of Saint La Salle is committed to providing quality, accessible, and transformative education that develops individuals who are competent, compassionate, and responsible members of society. We aim to nurture students who are grounded in values, guided by faith, and equipped with knowledge and skills for lifelong learning.

Our Vision

We envision a leading Catholic educational institution that produces globally competitive graduates who are committed to excellence, service, and leadership. We strive to form individuals who can positively contribute to society and respond to the needs of a changing world with integrity and compassion.

Core Values

Faith • Service • Excellence • Integrity • Compassion
