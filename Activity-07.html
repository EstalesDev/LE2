Adobo

Ingredients

  1. Chicken
  2. Soy sauce
  3. Vinegar
  4. Water
  5. Garlic & Onions

Cooking Steps

  1. Saute garlic and onions with oil
  2. Add chicken and let it fry
  3. Add water and let it boil
  4. Add soy sauce and seaoning (options)
  5. Let it cook and once ready you're done
